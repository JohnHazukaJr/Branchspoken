Ruth's first winter up north

About Ruth, Walter

Listen

Carol's voice · 4 min 10 sec

Transcript

Mom always said she got on the train in Chicago wearing the only wool coat she owned. It was the Twin Zephyr, and it left on January 14, 1952. She was twenty years old and had never seen snow that stayed on the ground for more than a day.

Dad met her at the station with a pair of his sister's boots because he knew she wouldn't have any. She said they were two sizes too big and she wore them all winter anyway.

They rented a room on Lyndale Avenue above a bakery, and she used to say the only reason she survived February was the warm floor and the smell of bread coming up through it.

She swore she would never get used to the cold. She lived in Minnesota for sixty-seven more years and never did.
